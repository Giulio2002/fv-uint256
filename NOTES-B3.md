## Recipes / pitfalls
- RecMem gained the ret-slot and saved-rbp invariants (RecMem.write has autoParam omega side goals); needed for `ReachesRet ... (retAddrOf s)`. Changing RecMem forces recompiling ALL G files (~30 min).
- gseg.py G_c49_d1b dropped the `>>>`/`<<<` operators when parsing: do not use it; RecipTail is hand-written (case rcx=0 vs 1<=rcx<64).
- sum2 now states R2 exactly (guard included); old disjunctive form could not give R2 = recipR2.
- Stage glue pattern: obtain vars from the sum lemma's existential, pass the exact L args (use `rw [ea0,..] at hL` to rewrite spill copies), feed `lohi` facts to the 4q/5q accumulation lemma.
- Never `omega` with the big stage context (use standalone lemmas, e.g. RecipMainLem.rcx_lt); `rfl`/`exact` between `2^64` and the literal works.
- `simp ... at *` and `assumption` on writeLE chains hang: use content-based `simp only [readLE_writeLE_of_disjoint, readLE_writeLE_self, Nat.mod_eq_of_lt hB]`.
- Light compiles run via lc.sh without heavy-run (G files ~35 s each); background them with nohup and poll with short loops (tool calls >120 s get backgrounded).
