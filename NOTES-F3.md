## Conventions worth knowing (arm64)
* tests are `tbz/tbnz` on bit 0 (Width/Precision: x1 `ok`; flags: x0), stepper condition forms `(getLsbD x 0 == true) = true`, `== false`, and with `^^^ 1#64`
  for the `eor x2,x0,#1` flags ('-', '#'): Facts `lsb0_iff/lsb0_false/lsb0_xor/lsb0_xor_false`.
* the verb switch (signed 32-bit compare chain) is decided by passing `addSub, addWithCarry, BitVec.msb_eq_decide, sext_trunc ch32 hlt` to janus_run.
* the `asr #63` of the `[2:]` offset is printed as the rotate form; `hexoff64` states it exactly.
* `ret` needs `CodePtrOK (S0.mem.readLE S0.sp 8)` (4-aligned, < 2^56): hypothesis `hret`.
* rename_i after janus_run is positional (linter warning only); the sites use `janus_grab MemFrame` where the order of keep/frame conjuncts varies.
* FInv holds S.sp = S0.sp as an equation; pass it (`i4`) to janus_run, and state spill facts in terms of S0.sp.toNat.
